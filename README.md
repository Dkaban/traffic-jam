# Traffic Jam

Build the roads. Keep the city moving.

Traffic Jam is a browser-based traffic management game where you design the streets and watch a city come to life. Place houses, apartments, offices, and shops to bring vehicles onto the roads, then find ways to keep traffic flowing as the neighborhood grows.

Draw new connections, rethink busy intersections, and experiment with traffic lights, stop signs, fast lanes, one-way streets, and roundabouts.

[Play Traffic Jam](https://dkaban.github.io/traffic-jam/)
