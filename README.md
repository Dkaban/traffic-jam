# Traffic Jam

Build the roads. Keep the city moving.

Traffic Jam is a small browser game about designing a road network and managing the traffic that comes with a growing city. Draw streets, place houses and businesses, and watch cars travel between destinations.

As you add more buildings, more vehicles take to the roads. Apartments bring extra traffic, and busy intersections can slow everything down.

## Play

[Play Traffic Jam](https://dkaban.github.io/traffic-jam/)

## How to play

- **Draw roads** by clicking and dragging across the map.
- **Add buildings** beside your roads to bring more cars into the city.
- **Manage traffic** with traffic lights, stop signs, fast lanes, one-way streets, and roundabouts.
- **Watch your city** grow and keep an eye on traffic flow.
- **Reset the city** to start over with the original layout, or create an empty city from scratch.

Your city saves automatically in your browser.

## Running locally

Run `npm run start` from the project folder, then open `http://localhost:8080`.

## Controls

Use the toolbar to build roads, place buildings, and change road rules. Scroll to zoom, right-drag to move around the map, and press **Ctrl/Cmd + Z** to undo. Select **How to play** in the game for more controls.
