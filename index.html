Draw streets, zone land and watch your city grow. Start with homes, then give residents places to work and shop.

01Draw roadsChoose Road and drag across the map. Intersections connect automatically.
02Paint zonesChoose residential, commercial or industrial and drag over vacant land beside a road. Buildings appear as demand grows.
03Supply utilitiesDrag a power plant or water tower to preview its 9 × 9 service area. Release to place it. New lots need both services, and the map marks buildings missing either one.
04Keep trips movingResidents drive to shops and jobs they can reach. Try different routes or traffic controls to ease queues.
